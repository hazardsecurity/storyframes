# Story: from a product to a 30–60 s film

## The one thing
Write one sentence the viewer must leave with. If it has "and" in it, cut. Everything in the film
serves this sentence; anything that doesn't goes.

## The spine: one person
Pick one person with a name, a job and a want: *Tolu, an Android developer at a 2,000-person
conference, wants a mentor.* Follow only them. Side characters exist to answer their want (Ada, the
mentor). Viewers project themselves onto one specific person far more than onto "users".

The arc in five beats (map them to scenes):

| Beat | Job | Typical length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hook | One striking image in the first 2 s; the world and its scale | 4–8 s |
| Tension | The problem, made physical and a little funny (the maths, the sweat, the clock) | 4–6 s |
| Turn | The product appears *as the character's idea* (phone out, a smile) | 2–4 s |
| Proof | The product working, step by step, in its own UI | 8–20 s |
| Payoff | The want, fulfilled, in the physical world (they meet, they shake hands) | 4–7 s |
| End card | Logo, one line, the URL, held | 4–5 s |

## Make the problem physical
Abstract problems ("networking is hard") don't animate. Find the physical version:
- a number that lands: *2,000 people × 2 minutes = 66 hours of small talk*; a clock spinning
- a crowd so large the character shrinks in it, head turning left, right, worried
- a wall of identical name badges; a pile of business cards
- before/after: the old way on the left, the product on the right, the split collapsing

## Devices for storylines (write three, recommend one)
- **A moment everyone knows** (a name badge, a queue, a group chat) carried by one object that
  transforms into the product. Object-to-object chain: every shot is entered through the thing just tapped.
- **A number lands**: one frame that rebuilds around a counter (rank #48 → #3).
- **Before and after**: split screen, the old way loses.
- **Two sides of a market**: two characters looking for each other (a hiring manager and a
  talent), intercut, meeting in the middle when both say yes.

For each storyline write: device, form, the first 2 seconds, the beats, the look, the sound.

## Honesty rules
- Only real features, real labels and real numbers. Demo data is labelled on screen.
- No invented testimonials, logos of companies that don't use the product, or "trusted by" claims.
- If you don't know a claim is true, ask, or leave it out.
